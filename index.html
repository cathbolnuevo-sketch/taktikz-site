    TAKTIKZ

    • ■ Square pieces (rows 1 & 8) move one square orthogonally — forward, backward or sideways.
    • ● Circle pieces (rows 2 & 7) move one square diagonally, sideways or backward — but never straight forward.
    • Capture by moving onto an enemy-occupied square — in any direction that piece may move. Nothing jumps.
    • White (rows 1–2) wins by landing a piece on row 8. Black (rows 8–7) wins by landing a piece on row 1. Circles get there by zig-zagging diagonally.
    • You also win by capturing every enemy piece, or if the opponent has no legal move. 100 moves without a capture is a draw.
    • Controls: drag a piece onto a highlighted square, or tap the piece then tap its destination.
